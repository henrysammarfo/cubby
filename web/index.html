How it works

Three steps. The chain itself checks that the approval is real.

1 Create a phone lock

Your face or fingerprint on this device becomes the only way to open the box.

2 Put coins in the box

Send Bitcoin Cash from Paytaca, Selene, Cashonize, or any wallet you already use.

3 Hold to open and send

Hold to review, then approve with your face. The chain checks it before coins move.

  2. Your face or fingerprint on this device becomes the lock for the box.

    i

    The lock is tied to this website address. A lock made on a preview URL will not work on the live site.

    Connect a wallet first, or skip ahead by creating the lock anyway.

  3. Send at least 20 000 units to the box, or paste a payment id.

    i

    The on-chain check is a large transaction, about 10 KB, so a smaller deposit cannot cover the send fee.

    Create your phone lock first so we know the box address.

  4. Send the coins back with your face or fingerprint.

    i

    You will hold to review, then approve on this device. The chain checks the approval before the coins move.

    Put coins in the box first.
